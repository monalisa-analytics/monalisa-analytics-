### 📌 Featured Projects

**[Healthcare Operations Analytics — Patient Flow & Resource Allocation](https://github.com/monalisa-analytics/Healthcare-Operations-Analytics)**
`Snowflake` `SQL`
Analyzed 9,216 patient records across 15 months. Found 59.3% of patients waited 30+ minutes and identified Neurology as the critical bottleneck (1 doctor, 241 patients, lowest satisfaction 3.54/5).

**[Pizza Sales Performance Dashboard](https://github.com/monalisa-analytics/Pizza-Sales-Analysis-Dashboard)**
`MySQL` `Power BI` `DAX`
Extracted and validated KPIs (Revenue, Orders, AOV, Peak Hours) in SQL, then rebuilt as an interactive Power BI dashboard with DAX measures and dynamic slicers.

**[HR Analytics — Employee Attrition Dashboard](https://github.com/monalisa-analytics/HR-ANALYTICS-PROJECT)**
`Python` `Pandas` `NumPy` `Matplotlib` `Seaborn`
Analyzed 311 employee records (37 features); identified low satisfaction and high absenteeism as the primary attrition drivers.

**[ESG Trends & Impact Analysis](https://github.com/monalisa-analytics/ESG-PowerBI-Dashboard)**
`Power BI`
ESG data trends and impact analysis built as an interactive Power BI dashboard.
